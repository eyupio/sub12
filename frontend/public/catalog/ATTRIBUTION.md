# Catalog Image Attribution

Every photo bundled under `frontend/public/catalog/rifles/` and `frontend/public/catalog/pellets/` must be listed here with its source URL, author, and license. **No image goes in without an entry below.**

Brand mark SVGs under `frontend/public/catalog/brands/` are generic text-based placeholders created in-house — they are not brand logos and are not derived from any trademarked artwork.

## Sourcing rules

Acceptable sources, in priority order:

1. **Wikimedia Commons** — must be CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, CC0, or public-domain. Record the file URL and the licence tag.
2. **Manufacturer press kit / media page** — only when the page explicitly grants redistribution. Quote the relevant clause.

**Do not** use:

- Retailer product photos (Pyramyd Air, Airgun Depot, Reloads UK, AoA, etc.) — copyrighted.
- Generic Google image search results — almost always copyrighted.
- Manufacturer images without an explicit redistribution grant — fair-use claims are too fragile here.

When unsure, leave `image_url` undefined in the catalog and let the brand fallback render.
